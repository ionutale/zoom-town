# Zoom Town

Zoom Town is a portrait-first 3D toy-town racing game for children aged 5–7. The car drives itself; the child steers, collects balloons, and boosts. Nobody loses, and progress stays on the device.

## Language

### Player & saves

**Profile**:
One child's save slot. A profile owns its stars, unlocked cars, best result, and tutorial state.
_Avoid_: account, user, player, save

**Avatar**:
The shape a child picks to identify their profile: star, flower, or moon.
_Avoid_: character, driver, face

**Driver**:
The animal character that belongs to a car: Bear, Bunny, Fox, or Cat.
_Avoid_: avatar, character, racer

**Car**:
A cosmetic vehicle paired with one driver. Cars differ only in shape, colour, and personality sound; they never differ in speed or handling.
_Avoid_: vehicle, kart, auto

**Garage**:
The screen where a child chooses an unlocked car.
_Avoid_: shop, selection, garage menu

### The race

**Race**:
One attempt at the circuit, made of two laps.
_Avoid_: run, match, level, game

**Lap**:
One complete pass around the circuit. A race is two laps.
_Avoid_: round, circuit, loop

**Track**:
One of five selectable toy circuits, including its road, theme, landmarks, and soft guardrails.
_Avoid_: map, level, course

**Guardrail**:
The soft edge of the track. It nudges the car back toward the road and never lets the car leave the circuit.
_Avoid_: wall, barrier, fence

**Balloon**:
A collectible floating on the racing line. Each balloon adds one boost charge and counts toward the race's stars.
_Avoid_: coin, pickup, collectible

**Boost**:
A two-second burst of extra speed, spent by tapping the boost button. One balloon provides one charge, and up to three charges can be stored.
_Avoid_: nitro, turbo, speed-up

**Rival**:
One of the three animal-driven cars that race alongside the child. Rivals are rubber-banded, never take balloons, and never block the road.
_Avoid_: opponent, enemy, AI car

**Bump**:
Gentle contact between the child's car and a rival. It pushes the cars apart without spin, damage, or speed loss.
_Avoid_: crash, collision, hit

**Auto-recover**:
The automatic return to the last safe point on the track after the car is stuck or facing backwards. It keeps the current boost and never costs a star.
_Avoid_: respawn, reset, teleport

### Progress

**Star**:
A rating of the child's best race result, from one to three. Stars are only ever gained, never lost.
_Avoid_: point, score, medal

**Result**:
The end-of-race summary: stars earned, balloons collected, and any car unlocked.
_Avoid_: score screen, summary, rewards

**Trophy**:
The one-time celebration for earning all three stars and unlocking every car.
_Avoid_: completion, medal, badge

### Guidance

**Tutorial**:
The short voiced introduction shown before a profile's first race. It demonstrates steering and boost with animated thumbs and can be skipped or replayed.
_Avoid_: onboarding, help, instructions

**Callout**:
A short spoken line that guides or celebrates, such as “Ready? Go!” or “Great driving!”.
_Avoid_: voice-over, narration, prompt

**Auto-steer**:
The temporary steering help that takes over after two seconds of wall contact, centres the car, and shows an AUTO badge.
_Avoid_: autopilot, assist mode, auto-drive

**Speedometer**:
The HUD gauge that shows the car's current speed in kilometres per hour.
_Avoid_: dial, speed display

**HUD**:
The always-on race display: the vertical track ribbon, balloon count, lap pips, boost meter, speedometer, and pause button.
_Avoid_: UI, overlay, interface
