# Art is built in code; audio is generated locally, not imported

**Status:** accepted · 2026-10-31

The project has no 3D artist and the house rules require original assets with one consistent hand. We build the toy town, cars, drivers, and props from Three.js primitives and custom meshes, and generate every voice, music loop, and sound effect on this Mac through the local `music-sfx-voice-studio` (Qwen3-TTS for the Italian and English callouts, Stable Audio 3 for music and organic SFX, procedural presets for clicks and beeps). Assets are committed under `static/audio` and played as local buffers; there is no browser speech synthesis, no cloud TTS, and no runtime audio fetch from a third party. This keeps the download tiny, avoids licence review, makes every colour and proportion tunable in one palette, and guarantees the game works offline. We rejected a CC0 racing kit, purchased/generated GLTF cars, browser speech synthesis, and cloud voice APIs because each adds licence, privacy, quality, or reliability risk without improving the toy-town look. The cost is a deliberately simple low-poly world and synthesized audio that is charming rather than studio-polished; the art direction carries the charm.
